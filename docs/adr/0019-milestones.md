# Three milestones, gated on measured cloud submission

M1: the owner applies from the cloud end to end, gated on at least 60 real applications, 85 % or more reaching sent, at most $0.03 per completed application, and zero wrong-company or duplicate sends. M2: invite-only beta with onboarding, mailboxes, Inbox and notifications. M3: public with billing. The unproven risk is cloud submission, not billing, so it is proven first.

## Considered Options

- Billing live from day one: spends effort where the risk is not.
