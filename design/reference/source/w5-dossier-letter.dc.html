Dear Northwind Labs hiring team,

I would like to apply for the Platform Engineer role. For the last six years my work has been the same problem your posting describes: keeping a high-volume intake path fast while the things underneath it change. At Redpine Commerce I led the migration of an order-intake pipeline from a monolith to Go services that now take 18,000 requests a second at p99 under 45 ms.

I built the multi-region read-replica routing behind that path, and the failover drills that keep reads correct when a primary goes down. The work is unglamorous and it is the part I am best at: three availability zones, one honest p99, and no pager going off for something a drill could have caught.

Your posting asks for someone who owns latency end to end. That is the part of this job I have done longest, and I would like to do it on a platform team that measures it in public.

Maya Okafor
