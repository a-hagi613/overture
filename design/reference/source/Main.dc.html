What it means

The O is the opening. The orange stroke is the one move Overture makes for you.

The same rule runs through the product: orange appears only at the moment Overture acts (Applying, Submitted, the active step, the travelling dot). It is never decoration.

Sizes in use

24 · 26 · 28 · 30 · 32 px (nav 32, sidebar 30, mobile 28, footer 26, rail 34).

Don't

Do not recolour the stroke. It is always orange.

Do not rotate or tilt the mark.

Do not stretch or squash.

No glow, gradient or shadow effects.

Do not place it on busy or coloured grounds.

OVERTURE

Do not change the wordmark typeface.

2Colour

Ink, paper and one orange. Greys are warm neutrals, not brown. The palette is called Ember and is the only palette. Dark mode is its inverse: ink ground, paper text. In v0.2 the dark ground is lifted one step (page #191714, card #221F1C, well #2A2723, muted text #ADA69B) so it reads softer without losing the ink feel. Small orange text on dark (signal-text) moves from #F0743A to #F37D44 to keep 4.5:1 on the lifted surfaces; the orange fill, ink and status hues are unchanged.

Orange marks only the moment Overture acts.

Applying, Submitted, the active step, the travelling dot, the Applied tray, the logo stroke. Not links, not hover, not focus, not decoration. Small orange text uses acct, never acc, because acc is 3.6:1 on paper.

Status colours

Hold · needs you Screening Interviewing Offer Submitted Closed

Statuses differ in lightness as well as hue. Hold is the only red. Applied and Submitted are the only orange states. On the Job Board the stage rule colours are: Hold hold, Applied acc, Screening, Interviewing and Offer ok, Closed mut. App chrome (selected cards, active tabs, meters, KPI numerals) uses ink, not orange.

3Typography

Three families, each with one job. Sizes are px at desktop; the 390 column is what changes on a phone.

Aa Aa

Instrument Serif · 400 and italic

Display, headings, big numerals, scores. A high-contrast editorial serif that carries the human, written-by-hand side of the product. Falls back to Newsreader, Georgia.

Aa

Plus Jakarta Sans · 400 500 600 700

All UI: body, buttons, labels, tables. Clean and round, reads well at 12 px. The precise, automated side. Falls back to system-ui.

Aa

IBM Plex Mono · 400

Timestamps, folio marks and "via Greenhouse" only. A typewriter note, sparingly, never for paragraphs.

Italic emphasis rule

One word or short phrase per headline is set in serif italic, at the end: "The job hunt, handled." Never a whole sentence, never in the sans. Italic is also used for scores, counts in trays, folio and figure marks.

Tabular numerals rule

Anything that counts, scores, dates or prices uses font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums (class .num): KPI values, Match scores, salaries, usage meters, tray counts, timestamps.
