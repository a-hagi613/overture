Questions

Does Overture edit my resume?

No. Your resume PDF is sent unchanged with every application. Only the cover letter adapts to each role.

What happens when it's unsure about an answer?

The application becomes a Hold instead of guessing. Answer once, it saves to your Answer Bank, and every waiting application moves forward.

Can I see what was submitted?

Yes. Every card on your Job Board opens a dossier with a dated Timeline, all form answers and their sources, the cover letter, the resume PDF, and recruiter emails.

Which job sites does it check?

Company career pages running Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, and Recruitee. Direct sources only—no aggregators or stale listings.

Is there a daily limit?

Yes. Standard workspaces include 25 applications and 75 Match Scores per day, resetting at 00:00 UTC. Free accounts include up to 5 applications per day.

Can I pause it?

Yes. Pause or resume auto-apply from Home at any time. No new applications are submitted while it is paused.
