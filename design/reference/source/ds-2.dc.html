Section rhythm (landing)

Sections are separated by space, not dividers: 96 px above and below (88 to 120 on the hero, how it works and band). The band is the only full-bleed colour change. Texture bookends: hero, and closing band plus footer as one block. Everything between sits on solid paper.

Breakpoints

Media queries are on the viewport: 480 (hide Sign in), 700 (nav row, tray labels), 767 and 768 (app phone layout, rail), 900 (connector becomes lists), 1099 and 1100 (bento and KPI), 1279 and 1280 (sidebar), 1700 (large screens).

Grain

An SVG feTurbulence filter (fractalNoise, baseFrequency .85, 3 octaves) tinted by a colour matrix, laid on a full-bleed layer that ignores the pointer. Light grain is warm grey (.30 .25 .20), dark grain is paper (.96 .92 .85).

Opacity: the grain tweak 0 to 1 maps to 0 to 0.24 in light and 0 to 0.15 in dark. Default 0.5.

Where: behind the whole page on Home and Job Board; on the landing only behind the hero and behind the closing band plus footer, fading out with a mask. Never over text: cards and sheets are solid paper above it.

Paper above grain

Opacity .12 behind a solid card.

Live demo · the dot

The travelling dot: orange, 11 px, 1.6 s on the shared easing, fading in and out at the ends. It is the one thing that moves while the page is idle.

Signature motions

NameWhereWhat happens
Sorting stackLanding heroEach card is one job and resolves at a timestamp between 11 pm and 7 am. Choreography per card: the top card lifts 6 px and its shadow deepens; the two evidence rows draw in one after the other (the dotted connector draws left to right); the receipt strip unfolds (.5 s); the ember Applying stamp presses in (scale 1.04 to 1, opacity 0 to 1, rotated -3 degrees); the card glides left and shrinks into the Applied count. A non-fit lifts, fades in its one-line reason and glides right, muted. Cards behind the top one sit smaller and dimmer. The tally "Applied 6 · Passed 3" ticks, a marker moves on the time rail, and at 7 am the morning summary card settles on top for about five seconds. One loop is 34 ticks.
Travelling dotWhere it appliesThe six systems sit in one tier, "Applies for you". Every connector carries a slow, continuous flow toward the hub (a dashed stroke whose dash offset animates, 1.8 s per dash period). On top of that the orange dot runs out to each tile in turn; the tile border turns orange and glows softly, a "Submitted" tag shows for about 1.5 s and the counter ticks. The hub has a very faint breathing ring (4.6 s) while active. On phone the connectors become a vertical spine from the hub with the dot running down it.
Reply readingEvery reply, read and sortedAn 18-tick sequence: a recruiter email slides in (10 px), Overture's label pill appears (Interview request), the matching board card lifts and slides to Interviewing (.7 s) with a "Moved to Interviewing" note; a second email crossfades in, is labelled Rejection, and its card slides to Closed with a Rejected tag. The Tracks tile uses the same card slide in a Kanban: one card moves from Applied to Screening every 8 s.
PathHow it worksA dotted path threads the six tiles in order through the gutters, with a numbered node at each tile. One orange dot walks it at about 520 px a second and rests .55 s on each node, which lights with an orange ring.

Reduced motion and the animate tweak

Every animation has a static state. With animate off, or under prefers-reduced-motion, transitions and keyframes are disabled and each loop shows one finished frame: a stamped top card with partial tray counts, the connector with all Submitted and Filled in labels visible, the path with no dot. State still changes; nothing moves.
