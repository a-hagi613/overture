Welcome to Overture

Here is what happens next, in three steps.

  • It prepares every application

  • You approve, then it goes

    Nothing is sent until you have read it and said yes.

Set up one Gmail filter

  1. 1

    In Gmail, open Settings, then Filters and Blocked Addresses, then Create a new filter.

  2. 2

    In From, paste these job-site domains. Leave every other field empty.

    greenhouse.io OR greenhouse-mail.io OR us.greenhouse-mail.io OR lever.co OR hire.lever.co OR ashbyhq.com OR workable.com OR workablemail.com OR personio.de OR personio.com

  3. 3

    Tick Forward it to, choose your forwarding address above, then create the filter. Gmail confirms the address with Overture automatically.
