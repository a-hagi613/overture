Every piece is drawn at its true pixel size and labelled with its file name. Mark geometry and colours come from the design system: ink #151311, paper #F3EEE4, ember #D2561F.

1Favicon set

At 16 px the full mark loses its fine ring, so favicons use a simplified mark: the ring and stroke thicken from 1.5 to 3 on the 32-unit grid. Shown at true size, on a light and a dark browser tab bar.
