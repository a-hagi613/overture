How it works
Step 1
Set your Targets
Roles, locations, workplaces and the salary floor you want. Overture matches Postings against them.
Step 2
Review what is Ready
Each Application is prepared with every answer, the resume and a cover letter where one is asked. You see the source of every answer.
Step 3
Approve the ones you want
Send goes out in daytime hours, 08:00 to 20:00 your local time, at least 20 minutes apart. Undo until sending starts.
Each application moves across the Board
The Board shows where every application stands with the employer, from Queued to Closed.
Queued
Being prepared, or waiting on a Review.
Applied
Sent to the employer, and confirmed.
Screening
The employer has replied.
Never charged for failure
An Application that can't be sent, or whose sending can't be confirmed, is marked Couldn't apply and never uses one of your applications. Only an Application confirmed sent counts against your plan. Applications left is always on Home.
